Where this art came from, and why these two kits.

Kenney Nature Kit          https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit          CC0
Kenney Food Kit            https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit            CC0

Both are CC0 - public domain, no attribution required. Credited anyway.

Picked for performance as much as for looks. The nature models carry no textures at all: they
are flat shaded and coloured by material, and the whole 329 model kit uses 23 material names
between them, so a map made of hundreds of props still runs on about ten materials and samples
no textures. The food models share one 10.7 KB atlas.

Measured before importing rather than after. The heaviest jungle model in the set is 564 faces
and most are between 200 and 400, so a few hundred props is on the order of a hundred thousand
triangles - nothing for a machine that runs this game at all.

The pineapple is 96 verts, 392 faces, one material, and 0.24 x 0.47 x 0.25m, upright on Y.
