Where this art came from.

Kenney Holiday Kit         https://kenney.nl/assets/holiday-kit         CC0

CC0 - public domain, no attribution required. Credited anyway (CREDITS.md). Downloaded 2026-09-30
as kenney_holiday-kit.zip, 4.5 MB, for the glacier map.

Models/ is 32 of the kit's models - only the ice and snow ones and the cabin pieces, none of the
Christmas ones (presents, candy canes, snowmen): snowy pines, snow-capped rocks, snow walls and
drifts, the log cabin set (walls, corners, doorway, windows, floors, snowy roof and chimney),
fences, lanterns, benches, sleds and two train wagons. Every model shares one 512x512 palette
texture, Models/Textures/colormap.png, the same atlas style as the food kit.

The glacier's own colours - the snow, packed ice and blue ice of the terrain, the frozen lake, the
sky - are read off that palette's swatches (GlacierBuilder lists which), so nothing on the map is a
colour the kit doesn't have.

Sizes, measured before building with them (at scale 1): the cabin pieces are 1m modules, a wall
1 x 1 x 0.3 on a tile's edge; a snowy pine 1.2 x 1.9; rocks-large 3.1 x 1.7 x 2.7; a snow wall
1.9 x 0.4 x 1.3. The map uses the cabins at three times that, which gives a doorway about 1.5m wide
and 2.4m tall - room for the player's 1m wide, 2m tall capsule.
