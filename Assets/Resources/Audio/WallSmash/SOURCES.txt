Three impactPlank_medium variants from Kenney's Impact Sounds pack, which is CC0 - no attribution
required, though the credits should say so anyway. The same pack this project's Shield, Footstep
and Impact banks already come from.

Plank rather than the pack's Metal/Glass/Plate variants - this game's arena walls are wooden
fencing (see the sandbox screenshots), so a plank thud reads as the actual material getting hit
rather than a generic clang standing in for it.

Two layers on a smash: this bank pitched up (1.15, ± a little jitter) for the main crack, and this
same bank again pitched way down (0.5) as a low body layer under it, falling back to Slam if this
folder is ever emptied - see PlayerMovement.WallSmash.
