Looping background for the map. Jungle: insects, distant birds, a bit of wind.

Wants to be long - a minute or more - and to have nothing distinctive in it. Anything with
a recognisable event (one loud bird, a monkey call) turns into a metronome once you have
heard the loop three times, and you will hear it three times in the first minute.

Quiet. It sits under the music and the gunfire and its whole job is to stop the map sounding
like a vacuum between fights.
