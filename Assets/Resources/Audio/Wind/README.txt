wind-whoosh-loop from "Wind Whoosh Loop" by SketchMan3, CC0 - no attribution required, though the
credits should say so anyway.

https://opengameart.org/content/wind-whoosh-loop

Made by the original author EQ'ing a section of another CC0 track (Loopable Dungeon Ambience) to
bring out the high frequencies - already processed for exactly the smooth-bed use this needed,
rather than raw nature recording.

Measured before trusting the description, the same lesson the cut jungle ambience left behind:
that track had wind chimes buried in it and measured 0.49 distinct events a second despite being
described the same way this one is. This one measures at 0.00/s over 6 seconds - AudioCheck's
WindIsSmooth holds it to that going forward, reusing the same CountOnsets the rejection was
originally measured with rather than a new method that could drift from it.
