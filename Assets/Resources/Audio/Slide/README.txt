Boots scraping along the ground. Drop one in here and it plays.

Wants to be short - under a second - and to be one continuous scrape rather than a sequence of
scuffs, because it is pitched up as the slide chain builds and a rhythmic clip turns into a
stutter when you speed it up.

With this folder empty the game borrows a footstep and pitches it down by nearly half, which
reads as a scuff and is obviously not the real thing.
