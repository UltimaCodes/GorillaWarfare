stoneDrag3 and stoneDrag4 from Kenney's Foley Sounds, which is CC0 - no attribution required,
though the credits should say so anyway.

Picked by measuring rather than by ear. All four drags in that pack are genuinely continuous -
their envelopes are sustained rather than a spike and a decay, which is what separates a scrape
from an impact - and these two are the smoothest of them: 0.53 and 0.58 distinct events a second
against 1.12 and 1.40 for the other two. Fewer events means fewer individual rock clacks, which
is what you want under a slide.

The measuring tool (ShieldSoundPick.cs, which covered this bank too) was a one-off and lives in
git history.

They are about 1.8 seconds each and SpeedRush loops one on a dedicated source rather than
retriggering it, with volume and pitch both following speed. Anything dropped in here should be
loopable for the same reason.
