Two lowFrequency_explosion variants from Kenney's Sci-fi Sounds pack, which is CC0 - no
attribution required, though the credits should say so anyway. Same pack this project's AirBrake
already comes from (thrusterFire).

Replaces the original pick (three impactSoft_heavy variants, from Impact Sounds) - reported
directly as "SO BAD, it sounds like someone hitting their microphone." A soft-material impact was
the wrong genre entirely for what this needed: a deep, weighty boom under the slam, not another
Foley thud sitting in the same boxy mid-range every other impact-pack sound already occupies. Only
two variants rather than three - the pack doesn't have a third - which is an acceptable trade for
actually sounding like the thing it's supposed to.

Plays at 0.4 volume for the wind-up (PlayerMovement.GroundSlamStartEffects) and at 0.85, pitched
to 0.8, for the actual landing impact (PlayerController.BuildGroundSlamImpact, reached over an RPC
so everyone nearby hears it, not just whoever slammed). Also the low body layer under a wall
smash's own crack (PlayerMovement.WallSmash) - already the heaviest, lowest sound in this game's
banks, which is exactly the shape a low thump under a different impact wants too.
