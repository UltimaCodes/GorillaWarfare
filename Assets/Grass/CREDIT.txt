Supplied by the project owner from their own patron download, 2026-09-27.

Terms, per the tutorial page above (paraphrased - check the page for the exact wording): usable in
free and commercial projects, modifiable, credit appreciated but not required, and not to be resold
on their own.

What's here, and what was left out of the download on purpose:
  Scripts/  GrassComputeScript, CullingTreeNode, ShaderInteractor, SO_GrassSettings, SO_GrassToolSettings
  Editor/   GrassPainterWindow (Tools > Grass Tool)
  Shaders/  GrassBlades.compute, GrassSurface.shader ("Custom/GrassComputeSurface")

  Not imported: ComputeGrassVertexFrag.shader (an older layout - its triangle struct doesn't match
  what GrassBlades.compute writes, so it would draw garbage), GrassSurfaceVR.shader (declares the
  same shader name as GrassSurface.shader), the ShaderGraph + Grass.hlsl pair (URP only), and
  RenderTerrainMap.cs (terrain-colour blending, unused - the BLEND toggle stays off).

Every change made to these files is marked with a "Gorilla Warfare:" comment, so they can be
re-applied on top of a newer download. See docs/bug-log.md's thirty-second pass for why each exists.
